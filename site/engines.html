Two engines, a dozen databases

Build two engines. Get every database in their families.

SchemaForge builds two full-feature engines, each once across every version of its database: engine_mysql and engine_postgresql. Every managed service, distribution and compatible database is a subset that falls right into its family: what it refuses is a declared fact, what it adds a typed table or a declared option. What looks very complex is solved by the design of the schema.

The families

Two full-feature engines. Every subset falls into one.

The plain engine has the most features; a managed service or a compatible database has fewer, and sometimes a few of its own. Building the full engine once, across every version, builds the schema of its whole family. Click any box.

engine_postgresql · next

PostgreSQL 15 to 18, and its family.

The same baseline and principles as engine_mysql, its own and far larger design: schemas, relations, types, domains, enums, ranges, sequences, functions and procedures, aggregates, operators and their classes, casts, collations, statistics, text search, policies, rules, triggers and event triggers, foreign data wrappers and servers, publications and subscriptions, extensions, roles and privileges. Amazon RDS, Cloud SQL, Amazon Aurora, AlloyDB, Spanner and CockroachDB fall into it as subsets. Objective #141.

How an engine plugs in

Five steps, each as its role.

1. The superuser

Makes the plugin's two roles, <namespace>_owner (no login) and <namespace>_service, and its schema, owned by its owner.

2. cortex_owner opens the cortex

Adds the plugin's row to cortex.plugins and runs cortex.open_to: its owner may see the cortex and reference the tables its own extend, nothing else.

3. The plugin's owner makes its tables

Its tables and functions, each a plug: id and design, UNIQUE (design, id), a foreign key to the cortex row it extends; and lets cortex_owner see them.

4. cortex_owner checks it

Loads its seeds (engine, versions, flavors, kinds, plugin_tables) and runs cortex.plug_in: a table that is not a plug, or not registered, is refused.

5. The plugin's owner fits it

cortex.fit_plugin: its service account the only writer of its tables, the cortex's accounts readers, every change logged, row-level security fencing it to its engine's designs.
