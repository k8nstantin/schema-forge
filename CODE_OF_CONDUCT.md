# Code of conduct

SchemaForge is built by people who are learning together: about databases, about coding with agents, and about
building in the open. Everyone is welcome, whatever they know today.

- **Be kind and patient.** Every question is a fair one; answer it the way you would have wanted to be answered.
- **Assume good intent,** and say what you mean plainly and respectfully.
- **Critique the work, never the person.** Reviews say what to change and why.
- **No harassment of any kind,** in any space of the project: issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews.

The maintainer may remove comments, and anyone who does not keep to this, from the project's spaces. To raise a
concern privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
